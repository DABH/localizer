Version 1, September 27, 2026

**In short:** you transfer the copyright in your contributions to Snizyx Software LLC, the company that
develops Localizer. You keep a license to use your own contributions however you like. Snizyx can license
the project under any terms, including the open-source license it is published under. This summary is for

This agreement is between you and Snizyx Software LLC ("Snizyx", "we"). It covers your contributions to the
Localizer projects that Snizyx maintains, including https://github.com/DABH/localizer (the "Project").

"You" means the individual who signs this agreement. If you sign on behalf of a company or another
organization, you confirm that you are authorized to bind it, and "you" means that organization.

## 1. Definitions

"Contribution" means any work of authorship that you submit to the Project for inclusion in it, such as
code, documentation, translations or images, including changes to existing material. Anything you clearly
mark as "Not a Contribution" when you submit it is excluded.

"Submit" means sending material to Snizyx or to the Project in any form, for example a pull request, a
commit, a patch, or an issue or comment that you intend to be included in the Project.

You assign to Snizyx, worldwide, all right, title and interest in the copyright and related rights in each
of your Contributions, effective when you submit it. Snizyx may register, enforce and license those rights
in its own name.

irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free and transferable license, with the right to grant sublicenses through
multiple tiers, to exercise that right. You also agree not to assert it against Snizyx or anyone who
receives the Contribution from Snizyx. To the extent the law allows, you waive, and agree not to assert, any
moral rights in your Contributions.

## 3. License back to you

Snizyx grants you a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, non-exclusive and royalty-free license to use,
reproduce, modify, distribute, sublicense and otherwise exploit your own Contributions for any purpose. This

Snizyx may use, modify and license your Contributions, as part of the Project or otherwise, under any terms
it chooses, including open-source and commercial licenses. Snizyx isn't obliged to use any Contribution.

## 6. What you confirm

You confirm that:

1. each Contribution is your original work, except for third-party material whose origin and license you

You will tell Snizyx promptly if you learn that any of these statements is inaccurate.

## 7. No warranty

Apart from what you confirm in section 6, your Contributions are provided "as is", without warranties or
conditions of any kind.

## 8. General

This agreement covers every Contribution you submit to the Project, including Contributions submitted before
you signed it.

You sign it by posting the comment that the CLA bot asks for on a pull request, while signed in to your
GitHub account. Snizyx records your GitHub account, the time, and a link to your comment.

If a court finds any part of this agreement unenforceable, the rest stays in effect. This agreement is
governed by the laws of the State of Wyoming, excluding its conflict-of-laws rules.
