The runtime libraries are free and open source. The hosted service, which translates your CLI's strings
and keeps them in sync through pull requests, is a monthly subscription for the GitHub account that owns
your repositories.

<Plans />

## How it works

- **A translation is one string translated into one language.** Setting up a CLI with 500 strings in 7
  languages uses 3,500 translations. After that, only new or changed strings count, which is usually a
  small fraction of the allowance. Translations are remembered within a repository, so the same string is
  never paid for twice there.
- **If a month's allowance runs out,** Localizer translates what fits, finishing languages one at a time,
  and continues after the allowance renews on the 1st of the next month. Unused translations don't roll
  over.
- **One subscription covers every public repository** of one GitHub account, personal or organization.
  Localizer serves public (open-source) repositories only.
- **Plans by size:** Solo is for personal GitHub accounts, Team is for organizations with fewer than 100
  employees, and Enterprise is for organizations of any size. See the [Terms of Service](/terms/).
- **Payments are handled by [Polar](https://polar.sh), our merchant of record.** Prices are in US dollars;
  sales tax or VAT is added where it applies. Polar sends your receipts and a link to its customer portal,
  where you can change or cancel your plan at any time. A canceled plan keeps working until the end of the
  period you paid for.
- **Enterprise** includes a 99.9% monthly uptime commitment with service credits (see the
  [SLA](/sla/)), support answered within one business day, and help with your security review.
